# Code of conduct

PCSS contributors and maintainers are expected to provide a welcoming,
harassment-free environment. Be respectful, discuss technical decisions in
good faith, accept constructive feedback, and avoid publishing another
person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical and reviewed without retaliation.
