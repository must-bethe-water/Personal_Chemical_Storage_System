# Scientific and safety disclaimer

PCSS is a personal inventory aid. It is not a safety data sheet system,
laboratory information management system, regulatory compliance system, or
authoritative chemical identity service.

Database matches and generated structures may be missing, ambiguous, outdated,
or incorrect. Users must independently verify chemical identity, concentration,
hazards, compatibility, storage requirements, disposal requirements, and
applicable regulations using authoritative records and current safety data
sheets. Never rely on PCSS alone for handling or emergency decisions.
